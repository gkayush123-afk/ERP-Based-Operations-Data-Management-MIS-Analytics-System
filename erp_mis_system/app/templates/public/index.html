About the project

From scattered records to a shared source of truth

Manual spreadsheets can lead to duplicate entries, missing information, inconsistent updates, and reporting that takes too long.

The challenge

Scattered Excel sheets make it hard to know which data is current. Missing records and manual consolidation can reduce confidence and slow down decisions.

The solution

A centralized system organizes operational data, checks its quality, supports review workflows, and prepares clear MIS reports from trusted records.
